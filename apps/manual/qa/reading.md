# Reading qualification

This isolated page exercises the actual manual renderer. It is only included by the
qualification build and is never indexed or part of normal navigation.

## Five meanings

> [!NOTE]
> Additional context.

> [!TIP]
> A useful alternative.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A prerequisite that must not be missed.

> [!WARNING]
> A consequence to consider before continuing.

> [!CAUTION]
> An action with an irreversible consequence.
